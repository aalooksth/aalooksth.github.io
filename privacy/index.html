Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026 • Committed to Client-Side Privacy & Transparency

Privacy Commitment Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to aloks.com.np (hosted via GitHub Pages as aalooksth.github.io) and all accompanying browser utilities. Your privacy and digital autonomy are paramount. All computational tools, calculators, calendars, and migration guides are designed to execute 100% client-side in your web browser without storing your personal data on any server.

Utilities & Tool-Specific Data Policies

🪪 Smart License Print Checker (/utils/license-print-check/)

  • Zero Server Logging: Entered driving license numbers and queries are processed entirely in browser memory. No license numbers are logged, collected, or stored in any remote database.
  • Direct Public Inquiries: Status checks query publicly accessible transport office records directly from your browser.
  • Local Search History: History is saved strictly in your device's localStorage and can be permanently wiped with one click.

📊 Nepal Income Tax Calculator (/utils/nepal-tax-calculator/)

  • Local Calculations: Salary amounts, SSF/EPF/CIT figures, insurance rebates, and tax comparisons are calculated on-the-fly using browser JavaScript. No financial data is ever sent across the network.

📅 Nepali Date Calendar & Festival Planner (/utils/nepali-calendar/)

  • Private Events Storage: All scheduled notes, birthdays, and custom reminders reside solely in your browser's localStorage.
  • Export/Import Portability: You have full control to export your schedule to a JSON file or import backups without cloud dependency.

Analytics & Telemetry

Standard anonymous analytics tools are utilized solely to monitor site health, traffic, and feature usability:

  • Google Analytics: Aggregates high-level metrics such as page visits, referring URLs, browser types, and general geographic regions. Visit Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Provides anonymous aggregated heatmaps to help identify UI bottlenecks and enhance site navigation. Visit Microsoft Privacy Statement.
